02כשאתם גולשים באתר

  • האתר מאוחסן אצל Vercel. כמו כל שרת אינטרנט, הוא רושם פרטים טכניים על כל בקשה: כתובת IP, סוג הדפדפן, העמוד שנפתח והשעה. הרישום משמש להפעלת האתר ולאבטחתו. אני לא משתמש בו לפרסום ולא כדי לזהות אתכם.
  • אני סופר ביקורים באתר בעזרת Vercel Web Analytics, כדי לדעת כמה אנשים מגיעים ומה עוזר להם. הכלי לא שומר עוגיות ולא שומר שום דבר בדפדפן שלכם. הוא רושם איזה עמוד נפתח, מאיזה אתר הגעתם, סוג המכשיר והדפדפן, המדינה, אילו חלקים בעמוד נצפו ואם נלחץ כפתור כמו וואטסאפ או סרטון. המספרים מצטברים ואינם מקושרים לשם, למספר טלפון או לכתובת מייל, ואין מעקב אחריכם באתרים אחרים. Vercel מעבדת את הנתונים בשרתים שעשויים להיות מחוץ לישראל.
  • אם תשנו הגדרה בתפריט הנגישות, היא נשמרת בדפדפן שלכם בלבד (localStorage) ולא נשלחת אליי.

This site collects almost nothing about you: it has no forms, no cookies and no advertising pixels, and visits are counted without identifying you. Personal details reach me only if you choose to write to me on WhatsApp or by email, and this page explains what happens to them.

01Who is responsible

02When you browse

  • The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, it records technical details of each request: IP address, browser type, the page opened and the time. These records keep the site running and secure. I don't use them for advertising or to identify you.
  • I count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, to know how many people come and what helps them. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records which page was opened, the site you came from, the device and browser type, the country, which parts of the page were viewed, and whether a button such as WhatsApp or a film was pressed. The numbers are aggregated and are not linked to a name, phone number or email address, and you are not followed to other sites. Vercel processes this data on servers that may be outside Israel.
  • If you change a setting in the accessibility menu, it is saved in your own browser only (localStorage) and is not sent to me.
  • The site's files, fonts and videos are served by the site itself. Nothing on the page loads by itself from an outside service.

03When you write to me

  • What I receive: only what you choose to send, usually a name, a phone number or email address, and your message.
  • Is it required: there is no legal duty to give these details; it is up to you. Without a way to reach you, I can't reply.
  • What it is used for: replying to you, preparing a quote, arranging a call or meeting, and providing the service if we work together.
  • If you ask me not to contact you again, I'll respect that.

04Where it is kept and who else sees it

Messages are kept in WhatsApp (Meta) and in my Gmail inbox (Google). These providers may store data outside Israel, for example in the United States or Europe.

I don't sell your details or pass them to anyone else for their own use.

05How long

I keep enquiry details for as long as they are needed to handle the enquiry and the business relationship, and as the law requires for business records. You can ask me to delete them sooner, unless I have to keep them.

06Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, you can ask to see the information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete information that is wrong, incomplete, unclear or out of date. Write to me on WhatsApp or by email and I'll get back to you soon.
